# shipmodel: Athena (IM-2) lander model

3D model of Intuitive Machines' Nova-C lander "Athena" (IM-2, March 2025), built from scratch in Blender 5.2
by a single script. No third-party meshes or textures are used; every part and material is generated by
[`tools/athena_build.py`](tools/athena_build.py).

## Files for other scenes

- `export/athena_lander.blend`: the lander only, in a collection named `Athena`. No lights, camera, ground or world.
  In Blender: *File > Append > athena_lander.blend > Collection > Athena*.
- `export/athena_lander.glb`: the same lander as glTF. The fine MLI crinkle is a shader effect and is not carried
  into glTF; the tank folds are real geometry and are.

Both: metres, Z up in the .blend. The origin (`Athena` empty) is the centre of the lander on the underside of the
footpads, so placing it on a surface puts the lander on its feet. Size: 4.43 x 3.88 x 4.30 m (leg span x leg span x height).

## Rebuild

```bash
blender --background --factory-startup --python shipmodel/tools/athena_build.py -- shipmodel/build 160
```

Writes `athena_lander.blend`, `athena_lander.glb` and studio preview renders (`studio_*.png`) to `shipmodel/build/`.
Pass `0` as the last argument to skip the renders. Copy the two lander files to `export/` when they change.

## What it is based on

| Item | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall height | 4.3 m | Intuitive Machines Nova-C page (<https://intuitivemachines.com/nova-c>) |
| Body | hexagonal, 1.6 m across | same page |
| Layout (white upper body, black lower body, body-mounted solar arrays, copper MLI tank, six three-strut legs) | from photos | Nova-C photo IM 00309 on Wikimedia Commons |
| MAPP rover | 45 x 38 x 40 cm | IM-2 mission descriptions |

Approximations and assumptions (not measured):

- The published 4.3 m is for Nova-C. Athena's own height was not checked separately.
- Leg span, body section heights, tank size and the positions of antennas and boxes are estimated from photos.
- PRIME-1 (drill and MSOLO), MAPP and the Grace hopper are simplified shapes; their mounting positions on the body are approximate.
- No logos, flags or agency marks are on the model.
