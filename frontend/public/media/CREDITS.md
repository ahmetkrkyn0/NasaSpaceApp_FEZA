# Media credits

Pre-rendered images and short loops shown in the interface as **illustrations**. They are not computed
data: the Sun, shadows, stars and lander in them were set in the renders for one recorded moment per
scene, not by the product's calculation, and the terrain shows a kind of ground, not the chosen site.
The product's computed Sun and Earth positions are in the horizon strip.

Every field must be filled before submission. `pending` marks a field that is not known yet.

## Moon surface scenes

(`poster.jpg`, `orbit.mp4`, `overview.jpg`, `ground.jpg`, `lander.jpg`).

## Earth globe

File: `stations/earth-globe/overview.jpg`.

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Prepared by | Berke Kuş |
| Tool | Unreal Engine 5.8 (offline render). Not Unity |
| Other tools used in production | Python scripts (`tools/ue_build_orbit.py`, `make_stars_clouds.py`, `make_sphere.py`); ffmpeg for the loops |
| Earth and Moon textures | Earth day: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation (April 2004, topography and bathymetry). Earth night: NASA Black Marble 2016 (SVS 30876). Moon: NASA SVS 4720 CGI Moon Kit (LROC colour map; normal map built from the LOLA elevation map). Clouds and stars: procedural (`tools/make_stars_clouds.py`), not real weather or a real star catalogue |
| Satellite model | TDRS-A model from NASA 3D Resources (<https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/>), enlarged so it can be seen |
| Scale note | Not to scale: the Moon is placed much closer to Earth than in reality and the satellites are enlarged. The render only illustrates which bodies are involved |
| Licence of third-party parts | NASA media usage guidelines for all NASA models and imagery (no endorsement implied) |
